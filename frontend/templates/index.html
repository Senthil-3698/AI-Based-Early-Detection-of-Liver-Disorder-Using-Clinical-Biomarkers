About This Project

Project Overview

Engineered an end-to-end ML pipeline benchmarking CatBoost, LightGBM, XGBoost, Random Forest, SVM, Logistic Regression, and MLP on liver disease datasets with automated evaluation dashboards.

Methodology

Documented complete methodology, experiments, and deployment workflow in a comprehensive project report, enabling reproducible research and streamlined handover for production deployment.

Dataset

Trained on 30,693+ patient records with 9 clinical biomarkers including enzyme levels, protein measurements, and demographic data.

AI Models

7 state-of-the-art machine learning algorithms with performance ranging from 58.92% to 99.74% accuracy.

Performance

Random Forest achieves the best results with 99.74% accuracy, 99.49% precision, and 99.99% ROC-AUC score.

Clinical Application

Designed for early detection and screening, helping healthcare professionals make informed decisions about liver disease diagnosis.
